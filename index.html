Module 1: Structural Anatomy

The biggest mistake beginners make is speaking to AI like a human search engine. AI requires structured syntax: Role, Task, Context, Constraints, and Format.

Bad Example:

"Write a blog about AI design."

MetaGie Structure:

"Role: Senior UX Researcher. Task: Write a 1000-word blog post. Context: We are explaining glassmorphism to junior devs. Constraints: Use markdown, bold key terms. Tone: Academic but accessible."
